The Process

Selling your phone,
simplified.

From filling the form to cash in your hand — here's exactly what happens, step by step, with nothing hidden.

Step by step

What actually happens.

01

📋 Fill the form (2 minutes)

Pick your brand and exact model, answer a short set of condition questions (screen, body, battery, water damage, functionality). You get a live, transparent price estimate before you commit to anything.

02

📞 We confirm & schedule pickup

Choose a morning or evening slot and share your address — no minimum distance, anywhere in Delhi NCR. We call once to confirm. That's it, no repeated follow-ups.

03

🚗 Rider inspects your device

Our rider verifies the condition, IMEI, and functionality against what you declared — typically within 90 minutes of booking. If everything matches, your quoted price stands.

Avg. 90 min pickup
04

💰 Get paid instantly

Cash or UPI, on the spot — no bank transfer delays, no waiting for "processing." The moment inspection is done, you're paid.

05

🧾 GST bill + IMEI certificate

Full legal documentation for every transaction, sent straight to your WhatsApp right after payment — so the sale is safe and verifiable on both sides.

Pricing methodology

How your price is calculated.

No black box. Your estimate starts from the real market price for your exact model, then adjusts for the details you give us — nothing is deducted at the door that wasn't already part of the number you saw online.

Base market price→ adjusted by →Storage variant→ adjusted by →Condition grade
Battery health(iPhone — affects payout)→+10% loyalty bonus(returning customers)
What our rider checks

Inspection criteria.

📱

Screen & body condition

Cracks, scratches, dents, and overall cosmetic wear graded against what you declared in the form.

🔋

Battery health

Checked against the device's own battery-health reading (iPhone) — factored transparently into the final number.

💧

Water damage

Verified against your declaration — no surprise deductions if it matches what you told us.

🔐

Biometrics, camera & IMEI

Face ID/fingerprint, camera, speaker, mic and buttons tested; IMEI checked and logged for your GST certificate.
