Privacy
Policy.

What we collect when you sell a device or create an account, and how we use it.

Last updated: 18 August 2026

⚠️ This is a plain-language draft describing what this website actually collects and how it's used. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer — have it checked against Indian data-protection law (the DPDP Act, 2023) before relying on it as your final policy.

1. Who we are

Sambhi Mobiles ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to buy used phones and sell certified pre-owned devices in Delhi NCR. This policy explains what information we collect through the site, why, and how it's stored.

2. Information we collect

When you use the "Sell Your Phone" form, we collect: your name, phone number, email (optional), pickup address, pincode, and area/zone; details about your device (brand, model, storage, colour, purchase year, IMEI, condition, battery health, accessories); photos of the device and, if provided, your purchase bill; and your answers to diagnostic questions (power, biometrics/camera, speaker/mic/buttons).

If you create an account, we additionally store your chosen password (as a one-way hash, never in plain text) and, if you sign in with Google, your name, email and Google account identifier.

We do not collect payment card details through this site — payment for devices we buy from you is made in cash or via UPI directly at pickup.

3. How we store your information

Some information (your session, saved form progress, and a local copy of your account) is stored in your browser's local storage. Sell-request details, including uploaded photos, are also sent to our database (hosted on Supabase) and photo storage bucket so our team can process your pickup. Account passwords are hashed before storage — we cannot see or recover your original password.

4. How we use your information

  • To calculate a price estimate for your device and generate the GST bill / IMEI certificate for completed sales.
  • To schedule and carry out free doorstep pickup.
  • To contact you about your sell request or account (by phone, WhatsApp, or email).
  • To detect returning customers and apply the 10% loyalty bonus automatically.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Third-party services

We use Supabase for database and file storage, and Firebase/Google for optional "Sign in with Google" authentication. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at mobilessambhi@gmail.com or +91 7827505579. Business records required for GST/tax compliance (such as invoices) are retained as required by Indian law even after a deletion request.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mobilessambhi@gmail.com or our contact page.
